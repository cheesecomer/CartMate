## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a calm, tactile, and highly organized culinary shopping experience. Built upon the architectural foundations of Material Design 3 and infused with modern soft-minimalist sensibilities, it replaces transactional coldness with warm domesticity, sensory delight, and effortless clarity.

### Target Audience & Mindset
- **Audience:** Everyday grocery shoppers, culinary planners, recipe curators, and organized households seeking a stress-free planning and buying workflow.
- **Emotional Response:** Serenity, domestic order, effortless competence, and fresh anticipation. Interacting with shopping lists and fresh produce should feel as wholesome as visiting a neighborhood morning market.

### Design Aesthetic
- **Soft Material You Hybrid:** Volumetric tonal layering, generous pill shapes, dynamic surface elevations, and tactile feedback.
- **Crisp Editorial Legibility:** High-contrast plum-slate text set against atmospheric lilac-tinted neutrals, elevated by pure white structural cards to give food imagery and list items maximum punch.
- **Fresh & Organic Sensibility:** Restrained botanical purple accents paired with airy padding and clean division lines, avoiding decorative clutter to keep tasks fast and accessible.

## Colors

The palette balances gentle botanical lilacs with deep structural typography and crisp surface containers.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#6B4E8C`):** Deep Lilac. Used for prominent active triggers, key navigation indicators, FABs (Floating Action Buttons), and critical batch actions.
- **Secondary (`#8B6DAB`):** Lavender Orchid. Applied to tonal chips, subtle filtering tags, secondary selections, and interactive state highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#507567`):** Sage Moss. A natural vegetable-tinted complement introduced for dietary badges (organic, gluten-free), inventory confirmations, savings alerts, and nutritional data points.
- **Neutral Core (`#635B69`):** Slate Plum. Anchors mid-tone borders, subtle metadata icons, disabled states, and outlines.

### Neutral & Tonal Surfaces
- **Canvas / Background (`#F9F5FD`):** The primary view surface; an ethereal lavender-tinted wash that removes the clinical glare of pure white.
- **Surface Container Lowest (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity white reserved for individual product cards, recipe panels, and active list items to pop against the canvas.
- **Surface Container Low (`#F4EEFA`):** Inset drawers, segmented controls, and soft section backdrops.
- **Surface Container (`#F0E8F8`):** Unchecked list row resting backgrounds, search inputs, and structural toolbars.
- **Surface Container High (`#EADBFA`):** Activated selection states, highlighted shopping tags, and badge backgrounds.

### Typography & On-Colors
- **On-Surface / Primary Text (`#241633`):** High-contrast midnight plum. Ensures crisp legibility across all ambient lighting conditions without the harshness of pitch black.
- **On-Surface Variant / Secondary Text (`#55495E`):** Soft plum-grey for unit metrics, weights, quantity increments, and subtotals.
- **Outline / Border (`#E1D5EC`):** Soft perimeter hairline for structural dividers and input outlines.

## Typography

The type system blends the characterful, contemporary warmth of **Epilogue** for display, category titles, and store banners with the fluid, highly legible geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for interfaces, product names, metrics, and checklist elements.

### Hierarchy Guidelines
- **Epilogue (Headlines & Editorial):** Employs tight letter-spacing to present grocery sections ("Organic Produce", "Bakery & Grains", "Weekly Basket") with an artisanal, boutique grocery editorial voice.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (UI, Labels, & Quantities):** Tuned for dense tabular and list scanning. Generous open counters ensure small weights (e.g., `450g`, `2.4 lb`, `$4.99/ea`) stay crisp on small mobile screens under aisle lighting.
- **Strikethrough Treatment:** Checked items transition from `title-md` (`#241633`) to `body-md` with `line-through` decoration in `#8B6DAB`, reducing visual weight without disappearing completely.

## Layout & Spacing

A responsive fluid grid governed by an 8pt base spatial increment provides dependable thumb zones and swift handheld scanning.

### Layout Philosophy
- **Mobile First Handheld Rhythm:** A 4-column fluid layout on mobile screens (`<600px`) using `margin: 1rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Grocery lists span single-column full-width rows, while item grids (e.g., quick-add aisle shelves) collapse cleanly to 2 columns.
- **Tablet Reflow (`600px - 1024px`):** An 8-column layout with `gutter-md: 1.5rem`. Enables a dual-pane master-detail workflow: list categories on the left (3 columns), live list/cart on the right (5 columns).
- **Desktop Canvas (`>1024px`):** A 12-column layout maxing out at `1280px` centered width with `margin-lg: 3rem` and `gutter-lg: 2rem`. Supports three simultaneous panes: Recipe & Department Catalog (3 cols), Active Dynamic Shopping List (6 cols), and Nutritional/Budget Summary Sidebar (3 cols).

### Element Gaps & Micro-Spacing
- `space-xs (4px)`: Micro-padding between badge icons and item tags.
- `space-sm (8px)`: Padding between adjacent ingredient pills and stepper control buttons.
- `space-md (16px)`: Standard structural gap between list rows, input fields, and card interiors.
- `space-lg (24px)`: Section-to-section rhythm within an active shopping list.
- `space-xl (40px)`: Macro margins separating major store departments and checkout milestones.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses Material Design 3 tonal surface tiers paired with soft, lavender-tinted ambient shadows. Avoid high-contrast black drops; shadows must feel illuminated by ambient lilac light.

### Surface Tiers
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Surface color `#F9F5FD`. Used for base viewport layers and scroll containers.
- **Level 1 (Tonal Elevation):** Surface Container `#F0E8F8`. No drop shadow. Outlined with 1px border `#E1D5EC`. Used for inactive cards, table headers, and categorized grouping panels.
- **Level 2 (Lifted Containers):** Pure White `#FFFFFF` container with ambient shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(107, 78, 140, 0.08)`. Used for interactive shopping list items, produce cards, and recipe summary modules.
- **Level 3 (Interactive Overlays & Steppers):** Surface Container Lowest `#FFFFFF` with shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(107, 78, 140, 0.12)`. Applied to active drop-down sheets, filter menus, and increment badges.
- **Level 4 (Floating Accents / FAB):** Color `#6B4E8C` with shadow: `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(107, 78, 140, 0.28)`. Applied to floating quick-add buttons and sticky checkout bars.

## Shapes

The system adopts Material You's expressive, organic shape language with full pill containers, rounded-xl structural modules, and softened corner arcs that evoke friendliness and ergonomic ease.

### Shape Hierarchy
- **Pill (Full 9999px / `1rem` base standard):** Standard buttons, search bars, filter chips, category pills, nutritional tags, and quantity increment steppers.
- **Rounded-XL (24px / 3rem scaled equivalent):** Primary product cards, recipe modal dialogs, and sliding bottom sheets.
- **Rounded-LG (16px / 2rem scaled equivalent):** Inset thumbnail graphics, recipe preview imagery, and category selection tiles.
- **Rounded (8px / 1rem scaled equivalent):** Selection check targets, stepper mini-buttons, and micro-badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (FAB & Main Checkout):** Full pill (`9999px`), solid `#6B4E8C` background with `#FFFFFF` text. Height `48px`, horizontal padding `24px`. High-emphasis hover deepens to `#573E73`.
- **Tonal Button (Category Switching & Quick Add):** Pill shape, `#EADBFA` background with `#241633` text and icon. Hover state tints to `#DFC9F5`.
- **Ghost / Outlined Button:** Pill shape, 1.5px border `#8B6DAB`, transparent background with `#6B4E8C` label.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Filter Chips:** Pill shape, `32px` height. Default state features `#F0E8F8` background with `#55495E` text. Active/selected state shifts to `#6B4E8C` background with `#FFFFFF` text and a leading checkmark icon.
- **Dietary Badges:** Pill shape, compact `24px` height, using tertiary Sage Moss `#507567` at 12% opacity with solid `#2D4F42` text.

### Shopping List Items
- **Row Architecture:** Rounded-xl white containers (`#FFFFFF`) with 12px horizontal padding and 8px vertical padding.
- **Unchecked State:** Left-aligned circular checkbox (`24px`, 1.5px outline in `#8B6DAB`), followed by product name in `#241633` (`title-md`) and department subtext (`body-sm`) in `#55495E`. Right-aligned stepper counter (`- 1 +`).
- **Checked State:** Checkbox fills with `#6B4E8C` containing a white checkmark. Item title receives strikethrough in `#8B6DAB` with text color muted to `#8B6DAB`. Background subtly shifts from white to `#F4EEFA`.

### Checkboxes & Steppers
- **Checkboxes:** Smooth pill-squared roundedness (`6px`). Border `#8B6DAB` when unchecked. Transition to filled `#6B4E8C` when active with gentle scale spring animation.
- **Quantity Steppers:** Pill-shaped pill capsules (`36px` height) with `#F0E8F8` background. Buttons (`-` and `+`) are circular with soft tactile press feedback, framing the numeral centered in `label-lg` (`#241633`).

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Search Bar:** Pill-shaped (`48px` height) resting on `#F0E8F8` with a leading search magnifying glass in `#6B4E8C`. Placeholder text set in `#635B69`. Zero border in resting state; gains a 2px focused ring in `#6B4E8C` over a `#FFFFFF` fill.
- **Inline Add Inputs:** Single line text inputs with a soft border `#E1D5EC`, transforming into a focused pill card with active submit icons on keystroke.

### Product & Recipe Cards
- **Product Tiles:** Rounded-xl containers (`#FFFFFF`) with Level 2 elevation. Top half dedicated to produce/item imagery (rounded-lg inset with 8px margin). Lower half details product title, unit price (`label-lg`), and an omnipresent pill-shaped quick `+ Add` button.
- **Floating List Action Bar:** Anchored to bottom on mobile (`16px` margin), styled as a full pill dock in `#241633` with `#FFFFFF` text, showcasing total estimated cost and an active item count in a `#EADBFA` lilac chip.
